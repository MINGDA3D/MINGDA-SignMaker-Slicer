MINGDA SignMaker V0.5.0 Official Release

- Reworked Settings into Shape, Machine, and Print tabs, clarified key parameter labels, and updated multilingual UI text.
- Improved editing and project workflows with reliable Ctrl+A/Delete/Backspace shortcuts, safer selection deletion, unsaved-project prompts, and fixed scale undo/redo after adding text.
- Improved 3D preview startup, U-shaped edge slicing with finer 0.05 mm transition layers, Prime Tower defaults, and Orca/Klipper G-code compatibility.
- Fixed DXF imports for block-based files, nested HATCH holes, and duplicate HATCH/curve boundaries.
