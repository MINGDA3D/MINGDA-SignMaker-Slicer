What's Changed

V0.6.6
- Fixed floating acrylic-face slots caused by independently nested SVG/DXF contours losing their supporting shell walls.
- Unified shell and flat-slot profile normalization across STL export and 3D Preview, including collective nesting and split offset islands.
- Added focused geometry regressions and verified 1,361 top-slot components across 277 SVG/DXF files with no fully floating components.

V0.6.5
- Added Basic, Advanced, and Expert interface levels for progressively exposing settings and editing tools.
- Added project complexity detection, one-click level upgrades, persistent level selection, and full multilingual support.

V0.6.4
- Updated the MINGDA AD-F4 Mini no-print zone; previous built-in defaults update automatically.
- Updated the online tutorials, including decorative-panel and solid-letter workflows.

V0.6.3
- Fixed sharp multi-wall corner gaps in 3D Preview and STL export.
- Improved STL reliability for complex DXF/SVG artwork and long file names in Shape List.

V0.6.2
- Updated no-print zones for MINGDA printer profiles.
- Improved parameter-panel display and printer-profile synchronization on small screens.

V0.6.1
- Renamed the slot and V-groove speed setting for clearer process control.
- Updated the new terminology across all supported languages.

V0.6.0
- Added System, Light, and Dark theme options.
- Added Open Recent and moved the U-shaped letter profile into Custom wall presets.
